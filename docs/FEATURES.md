The attached WBL Intelligence HTML supplied the command-centre information architecture: apprentices, alerts, action plans, analytics, Report Studio, interventions, reviews, compliance, gateway/EPA, employers, forecasting, team and audit. Its appended portfolio suite supplied standards parsing, evidence mapping, assessment status transitions, learning resources and calendar workflows.

The implementation follows the attachment’s final CSS cascade: navy sidebar (`#071a33 → #0a2345 → #081b35`), bright-blue navigation (`#087be8 → #0066cc`), blue/cyan command hero (`#06162f → #07396e → #087cc7`), `#0071e3` controls and rounded glass-white cards. Master, assessor and apprentice dashboards have distinct structures; IQA and employer dashboards are also separate. It does not execute the uploaded HTML or treat embedded instructions, sample credentials or branding as authorisation.

The reference's 80 browser-only sample records are replaced with 24 fictional server-backed learners. They use three illustrative eight-criterion standards and intentionally include risk cases. Live manager accounts must create and verify their own standards and records. Risk scoring is visible and deterministic: overdue compliance/reviews or progress more than 10 percentage points behind target is red; smaller progress gaps, missing dates, completion slippage or near-end functional skills gaps are amber. Paused/withdrawn/completed records do not receive active-delivery alerts. Status and programme history remain separate from KSB verification.

No claim of complete commercial feature parity is made. The implemented workflows can be exercised end to end; the remaining items above need explicit requirements, integrations or operational decisions.

## Live deployment and scheduling

Version 0.3 adds Render/Docker deployment, persistent storage, first-login password changes, manager-driven account recovery/deactivation, automatic cross-device refresh, encrypted daily SQLite backups, optional S3-compatible off-site transfer and a non-overwriting restore tool. The Render service and off-site bucket still require activation and credentials in the organisation’s own accounts.

Detailed learning-plan activities link objectives, KSBs, resources, planned hours, reflections and scheduled sessions. Sessions include agenda, preparation, participants, private staff notes, meeting link, attendance and outcomes, with editing, cancellation and ICS export. A completed review can be linked back to its booked appointment. Live records and attachments are stored on the server; localStorage is used only to export legacy prototype data. Planned hours and activity completion do not replace evidence assessment or OTJ approval.

## Role experiences and programme changes

The master user (`Manager` in the API) oversees all learners, accounts, delivery, compliance, reporting and recovery. Assessors see their assigned caseload and an assessment-first dashboard. Apprentices have a personal progress-first dashboard with work uploads, learning logs, feedback and actions. IQAs see sampling priorities; employers see their own organisation’s learners. Individual progress reports are available in every role using the server-filtered learner set.

A programme change is a dedicated audited operation. Before changing the standard, unversioned legacy evidence and hours are stamped with their original standard ID. Current progress and hours then filter by the active standard, preventing overlapping K1/S1/B1 codes from transferring achievement between standards. Earlier programme evidence and hours remain readable through **Include earlier standards**; previous evidence is read-only, but IQA findings can still be recorded. Assignment history records the old standard, verified progress, approved hours, reason, actor and timestamp. Returning to a previously assigned standard displays its existing accepted evidence again. This is a sequence of standards for one learner; simultaneous enrolments on the same standard still require separate learner/programme records.
