# Apprentice support, assessor 1:1s and dashboard success stories

## Edit an apprentice

Open an apprentice → Edit apprentice. Programme details stay in the **Programme** tab. **Personal & ALS** adds the home address, date of birth, additional-learning-support status and record, reasonable adjustments/support plan and a support-review date. A future date of birth is rejected. Switching tabs keeps unsaved form values until the apprentice is saved or the editor is closed.

**Documents** uploads certificates, ALS assessments/diagnoses, medical information and other supporting documents. Choose a category and up to five files (5 MB each), then save the apprentice to upload them. Existing documents show their category, upload date and download/remove controls. Removing a document hides it from current access; historical backups/snapshots can retain previous records.

These details and documents are private to the master administrator and the apprentice's current assigned assessor. IQA, employer, apprentice and other-assessor accounts cannot read them. Supporting documents use a separate protected storage table and download endpoint rather than the general portfolio/resource-file endpoint. Downloads are attachments with private/no-store caching and sandbox/nosniff headers. The records and files are stored in the persistent database and its encrypted backups. They are not included in Outlook event descriptions, public resources or success stories automatically.

## Assessor 1:1s — master administrator

Open **Assessor 1:1s** in the navigation. Choose an assessor or view the whole team; record a 1:1 with its date, status, summary, caseload/performance and quality discussion, wellbeing/workload, development/CPD, agreed actions, manager notes and next meeting date. Planned/draft records can be saved before the meeting. Completed records require a summary and cannot be dated in the future.

Team cards and conversation history make it possible to review an individual assessor. Follow-up counts use the most recent record for each assessor. Open a record to view or edit it. Previous versions are retained when it is edited. 1:1 records and their history are master-admin-only; even the assessor concerned does not receive these private management notes through their dashboard or export.

## Success stories — master administrator

Open **Success stories**. Choose Learner success or Assessor success, enter the person's name, title, achievement, story and optional quote, and review the live preview. Save a draft, or select **Publish to all assessor dashboards** and publish. One spotlight is published at a time. Publishing another story replaces the previous dashboard spotlight; drafts remain private to the master admin. Uncheck publish and save to take the selected story off dashboards.

Every assessor sees the current published spotlight on their dashboard at sign-in, regardless of caseload; already open dashboards refresh through the normal workspace update cycle. The celebratory card includes the achievement, quote, person and story, with an expandable longer story. It uses only the text explicitly published by the administrator. It does not copy private learner/support or staff-supervision details.

## Reusable learning activities

In a learning plan, **Reusable learning activities → Saved learning activity** is now a dropdown of templates for that apprentice's current standard. Select one to preview its method, description, mapped criteria and planned hours, then choose **Use selected activity**. Review dates and wording before saving the new activity. Changing apprentice resets the selection; templates from other standards are excluded. Saving activities as reusable templates remains available on the learning-plan cards.
